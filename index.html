Can I see a demo first?+

Yes. Tell us about your business and we can start with a free personalized demo concept, with no commitment.

Do you work with businesses outside Europe?+

Yes. ByteBuilt is based in Tirana and works remotely with businesses worldwide, including outside Europe.

Will my website work on phones?+

Yes. Mobile comes first, with responsive layouts that adapt to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

How long will my project take?+

Projects typically take around 2–4 days, depending on the agreed scope and the content available. We’ll confirm a realistic schedule before starting.

What about hosting and domain costs?+

We’ll discuss the domain, hosting and any third-party requirements separately so the full scope is clear before you commit.
